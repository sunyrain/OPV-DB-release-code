Historical search definitions supplied by the authors

The six rows preserve the supplied query strings, source labels, total_results,
complete_status and last_updated. WOS uses TS syntax. Rows labelled Elsevier use
TITLE-ABS-KEY syntax. Named materials are optional OR terms, not mandatory
requirements in every hit. Queries also contain ternary, indoor, tandem and
other broad terms; retrieval eligibility is separate from final device scope.

Publication filters cover January 2025, 2025, 2025-2026, and 2020-2024 as written.
last_updated is a stored update timestamp, not a verified execution date.
total_results is the stored hit count for that definition, not a count of unique
included papers. Results overlap and must not be summed. complete_status=1 is
preserved as an original status code; it does not prove every retrieval request
succeeded. The separate request history includes access-denied attempts.
These six definitions are not a complete history of acquisition before 2020 or
of additional reference datasets and supplementary papers.
